To the ones who keep going,

We are SHAKE — Start Hustling And Kill Everything. We were founded by a group of friends who live by one motto: “4's up, L's down.”

That bond is at the heart of everything we make. We create exclusive, authentic clothing inspired by the camaraderie of lifelong friends, with a mission to bring you high-quality, luxurious streetwear.

The dragonfly in our logo stands for change, adaptation, and self-realization. To us, real change is the mental and emotional growth that helps the deeper meaning of life come into view.

Thank you. Real hustlers are always welcome.

— The SHAKE Family
